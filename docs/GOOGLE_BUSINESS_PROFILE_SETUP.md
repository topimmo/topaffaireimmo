# Google Business Profile Setup Guide - TopAffaireImmo

## Why Google Business Profile is Critical for Local SEO

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is **essential** for local SEO and visibility in Morocco. Benefits include:

- ✅ Appear in Google Maps
- ✅ Show up in "Local Pack" (top 3 results with map)
- ✅ Get customer reviews and ratings
- ✅ Display business hours, contact info, photos
- ✅ Free advertising in Google Search and Maps
- ✅ Insights about how customers find you
- ✅ Mobile-first visibility

**For Real Estate:** Google Business Profile can increase website visits by 30-50% and generate direct customer inquiries.

## Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

### Phase 1: Create Your Profile (30 minutes)

#### 1. Access Google Business Profile
1. Go to: https://business.google.com
2. Click **"Manage now"** or **"Create a profile"**
3. Sign in with Google account (use company email if possible)

#### 2. Enter Business Information

**Business Name:**
```
TopAffaireImmo
```

**Business Category:** (Choose primary category)
```
Real Estate Agency
```

**Alternative categories to add:**
- Real Estate Service
- Real Estate Consultant
- Property Management Company
- Real Estate Rental Agency

#### 3. Choose Business Location Type

**Option A: If you have a physical office**
- Select: "Yes, I have a location customers can visit"
- Enter full address
- Add service areas (cities you serve)

**Option B: Online-only business (Recommended for TopAffaireImmo)**
- Select: "No, I don't have a location customers can visit"
- Select: "I deliver goods and services to my customers"
- Add service areas:
  - Select "Morocco" as country
  - Add specific cities:
    - Casablanca
    - Rabat
    - Marrakech
    - Tanger
    - Agadir
    - Fès
    - Meknès
    - Oujda
    - Tétouan
    - Kenitra
  - Or select: "I serve customers all across Morocco"

#### 4. Add Contact Information

**Website:**
```
https://www.topaffaireimmo.com
```

**Phone Number:** (Add your business phone)
```
+212 [Add phone number]
```
*Format: +212 (country code) followed by local number*

**WhatsApp Business Number** (if available):
```
Same as phone or separate WhatsApp Business number
```

#### 5. Finish Setup
- Click **"Finish"**
- You'll be redirected to your business profile dashboard

### Phase 2: Verification (1-2 weeks)

Google must verify you own the business before it goes live.

#### Verification Methods Available:

**1. Email Verification** (Fastest - if eligible)
- Google sends verification code to your email
- Enter code in dashboard
- Verified in minutes ✅

**2. Phone Verification** (Quick - if eligible)
- Google calls or texts verification code
- Enter code in dashboard
- Verified in minutes ✅

**3. Postcard Verification** (Common for new businesses)
- Google mails postcard to business address
- Takes 5-14 days to arrive
- Enter code from postcard
- Profile goes live

**4. Video Verification** (New option)
- Record a short video of your business
- Google reviews within 3-5 days

**5. Instant Verification** (If already verified elsewhere)
- If you verified your website in Google Search Console
- May be automatically verified

#### Steps to Request Verification:
1. In Google Business Profile dashboard, click **"Verify now"**
2. Choose available verification method
3. Follow on-screen instructions
4. Wait for verification (email/phone: minutes, postcard: 5-14 days)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
1. Use keywords in business name (e.g., "TopAffaireImmo Best Real Estate Morocco")
2. Create multiple profiles for same business
3. Use fake reviews
4. Choose wrong category
5. Leave profile incomplete
6. Ignore customer questions/reviews
7. Stop posting after initial setup

✅ **Do:**
1. Use exact business name
2. Maintain one accurate profile
3. Earn genuine reviews from real customers
4. Choose most specific category
5. Complete 100% of profile
6. Respond to all reviews and Q&A
7. Post regularly (weekly)
